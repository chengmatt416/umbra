# Brag Plan (feature tour): Umbra

*(/brag, full workflow, run 2. The first run, `../brag-output/`, is the 22.5 s teaser. This run answers "introduce every feature, and keep it polished".)*

## What is this app?
Umbra is an autonomous agent that lives on your Android phone: it operates apps, builds and tests code in a real Linux on the same device, checks its own work, and asks before anything risky.

## The angle
Same deadpan premise as the teaser (*you gave an AI your phone; it asks first; the one thing it can never do is answer its own question*), but the middle is the whole product: **thirteen features, one scene each, every scene showing the real app doing it**, in three acts: *Do the work · Keep it running · Stay in control*. The colour does the story: acts 1 and 2 are the light porcelain, act 3 flips to the dark night colour at the approval, and the punchline stays dark.

## Why it is longer than 15–25 s
The skill's creative laws say "not one second more without a reason". The reason is the brief: *introduce every feature*. Thirteen features at the reading floor (≥ 2.7 s settled each: a short title plus one line of ≤ 9 words) cannot fit in 25 s without cutting copy below the readable floor. So: **55.0 s** on a 6-beat grid (3.3 s per feature), hook 4.4 s, punchline 4.4 s, lockup 3.3 s. Still one idea per scene, still no flashing text.

## Hook (0-4.4 s)
"You gave an AI your phone." then "Here is everything it does." (crescent mark behind, the phone rises on the last beat).

- Music cue guidance: every scene cut is on a bar line of the 3.3 s grid; strong cues: the phone rise (3.85 s), the colour flip (33.5 s), the punchline (47.3 s).
- Audio-reactive: the background glows breathe with the bed's low and high bands (pre-extracted). No waveform visuals.
- SFX (Kenney CC0, from the skill): `card-slide-1` quietly on each feature change, `impactSoft_medium_002` on the flip, `click_002` on the approval, `bong_001` on the punchline, `impactBell_heavy_000` on the logo.
